# Terms of use of Personas online

Personas online has synthetic personas react to an idea, an offer or a price. It is a filter for bad ideas and a generator of objections, not an oracle: results do not predict a real market.

## Access

- The service is **invitation-only**: there is no open sign-up. You sign in with a magic link (e-mail) or with GitHub.
- You are responsible for your account. Tell the operator if you think someone else has used it.

## Your AI key and your costs

- Studies run with **your own key** at the provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter). Calls are billed by that provider, on your account with it, under its prices and terms.
- The cost shown before each study is an **estimate**. The per-study and per-month caps limit the spending started by the service; they do not replace the limits you can set at the provider.
- Revoke your key at the provider and delete it in "My AI key" if you think it has leaked.

## Your content

- You remain the owner of your projects, pitches and studies. The service only uses them to show them to you and to run your studies.
- Only put in what you have the right to use: no personal data about other people, no illegal content, no attempt to misuse the service or the models.
- Export regularly what you want to keep.

## Service

- The service is provided as is, with no guarantee of availability or results. It may be interrupted, changed or discontinued; if it is discontinued, you will be told in time to export your data.
- The operator may suspend an account that breaches these terms or puts the service at risk.
