# Personas Hub terms of use

The Personas Hub is a public library of modules and country packs for the Personas simulator. These terms apply to browsing the Hub and to using an account.

## Accounts

- Accounts are **invitation-only**: there is no open sign-up. You sign in with a magic link (e-mail) or with GitHub.
- You are responsible for your account and your personal tokens (`pst_…`). Revoke any exposed token.
- Your account has a personal namespace. It can be renamed once, as long as it holds no publication. The `personas` namespace is reserved for official modules.

## Publications

- Only publish content you have the right to publish: the figures and tables of a module are cited facts or aggregates, with their sources.
- Unless the file says otherwise, a module or pack is published under **CC-BY-4.0** (CC0 is possible). A fork keeps the attribution of the original.
- **A published version is immutable and stays published**: studies are pinned to it and must remain reproducible. To fix something, publish a new version.
- A publication is visible right away with the "unverified" badge, then reviewed. A security lint rejects, among others, instructions addressed to the model (prompt injection), URLs and markup in labels, and undeclared sensitive attributes.
- Forbidden: illegal, malicious, discriminatory or misleading content, distributions rigged to bias a study, infringement of other people's rights.

## Moderation

- Anyone can report a module, a pack or a review. Three distinct reports set a version aside ("flagged") until it is reviewed.
- Moderation can verify, flag, yank from the index (the version stays downloadable by its exact reference) or remove a version (illegal or malicious content: download refused), and hide a review.

## Liability

The Hub is provided as is, without warranty. Synthetic personas filter out bad ideas and surface objections; they do not predict a real market.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time (`DELETE /v1/me`, or from your account page). Your published versions are kept and attributed to "deleted author". See the privacy policy.
